Chronicles of Taste

Our Story & Generational Lineage

Chapter 01

Ancestral Roots & The Khari Baoli Legacy

Founded on recipes passed down through three generations of master royal chefs, Dāwat preserves the endangered art of slow cooking. We reject commercial spice packets; our saffron originates in Kashmir's Pampore valley, and whole spices are crushed daily using heavy stone mortars.

Chapter 02

The Sacred Art of Dum Pukht & Clay Ember Fire

Our kitchen is built around two ancient anchors: dough-sealed deghs capturing delicate steam for our Awadhi Biryanis, and traditional clay ovens fired with pure charcoal. Our signature Dal Dāwat simmers for 24 continuous hours with churned white butter.
